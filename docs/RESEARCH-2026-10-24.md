# Storefront reference research — 2026-10-24

Purpose: identify useful shopping patterns for two body tattoo B2C shops and one lash/makeup tools shop. These are inputs to an original design, not layouts to copy. Findings below distinguish observations from our recommendations. No competitor conversion, accessibility score, or loading benchmark was measured.

## Local references supplied by the owner

| Reference | Observed strengths to learn from | Our design response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MRT](https://mrttattoo.com/) | Deep equipment/type/brand navigation; distinct cartridge configurations; campaign imagery and dedicated product merchandising sections. Desktop inspection showed a product-image-led hero and an initial notice overlay. | Retain specialist catalog clarity. Keep header text readable over any artwork, show fewer purposeful homepage sections, and avoid an entry-blocking notice. Do not inherit their taxonomy wholesale. |
| [Artoo](https://artooshop.com/) | Product-group entry points, links to buying/shipping/returns/order tracking, and editorial guides. Desktop inspection showed a strong dark header and shaped campaign artwork. | Retain routes to products and help. Use a cleaner visual hierarchy with consistent controls and real text outside banner images; do not copy the visual shell. |
| [Boom](https://boomshopp.com/) | Prominent product search, body/face entry points, support and tracking in the navigation. Desktop view used dark/green styling with several campaign panels. | Familiarity, search and reassurance are useful candidates. Keep body tattoo as the primary taxonomy for our tattoo shops. The owner's report that customers like Boom does not establish which feature causes that preference; validate with real shopper tasks. |

Inspection: fetched homepage/catalog text and visually reviewed each homepage in a desktop browser. Some secondary Boom requests timed out. Selected MRT/Artoo product pages were fetched, but the extracted content did not support a complete product-page audit. This is reference research, not a functional or security audit of those businesses.

## International category references

- [Killer Ink homepage](https://www.killerinktattoo.co.uk/) and [cartridge category](https://www.killerinktattoo.co.uk/tattoo-cartridges): specialist browse paths by type/brand/configuration, with cartridge comparison and buying guidance. Our takeaway: expose the attributes that decide the purchase and connect guidance to the catalog. Do not copy a large international catalog's entire navigation or its price/tax rules. A comparison tool needs complete, comparable product data before it is useful.
- [Barber DTS](https://www.barberdts.com/uk/): deep equipment/brand families. Our takeaway: serve experienced repeat purchasers with direct catalog routes and clear product organization. Regional delivery/payment claims on another store are not promises our stores can make.
- [LashBase](https://lashbase.co.uk/) and [tweezers collection](https://lashbase.co.uk/collections/tweezers): navigation differentiates lashes, adhesives, tweezers and essentials; category guidance differentiates techniques and tool purposes. Our takeaway: the beauty shop needs its own attributes and visual language, rather than tattoo categories renamed. Confirm which of those product families this specific client actually sells.

## Research-backed interaction principles

| Topic | Evidence | Planned application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listings and filters | [Baymard product-listing guidance](https://baymard.com/blog/product-listing-page-plp-ux) discusses relevant filters, desktop/mobile filter presentation and applied-filter visibility. | Desktop filter panel; mobile drawer with explicit apply/clear and result counts; category-specific attributes; preserve the shopper's place when returning from a product. |
| Guest checkout | [Baymard account-selection research](https://baymard.com/checkout-usability/benchmark/step-type/account) identifies discoverability problems with guest checkout. | Clearly offer guest purchase; account creation can follow a successful purchase. Phone/OTP login is optional until a provider and business need are confirmed. |
| Carousels | [W3C carousel guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/carousels/) requires keyboard access, sensible focus and movement controls. | Static hero is the default. A brand can choose a slider with clear controls and pause support; avoid autoplay by default. |
| Touch targets | [WCAG 2.2 target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum) explains the 24 CSS-pixel minimum and exceptions. | Aim for 44px primary touch controls as our design target; do not misstate that as the universal AA minimum. Include visible focus and comfortable spacing. |
| Performance | [Google Core Web Vitals](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/core-web-vitals) covers loading, interaction and layout stability. | Target LCP ≤2.5s, INP ≤200ms, CLS ≤0.1 in field measurements at the 75th percentile; local preview results are not production evidence. |

## WordPress/WooCommerce implementation evidence

- [Classic theme development](https://developer.woocommerce.com/docs/theming/theme-development/classic-theme-developer-handbook): hooks and theme template overrides allow custom presentation. Prefer hooks; preserve commerce hooks when an override is necessary and track upstream template changes.
- [Product brands](https://woocommerce.com/document/managing-product-taxonomies/product-brands/): native brand assignments and archives can supply the directory/detail experience. Reuse this rather than adding a duplicate brand taxonomy or another brand plugin by default.
- [Cart/Checkout extensibility](https://developer.woocommerce.com/docs/block-development/cart-and-checkout-blocks/extensibility-getting-started): block checkout has its own extension mechanisms. A classic PHP product override does not redesign a block checkout; choose the supported extension path for the actual gateway and page implementation.

## Synthesis

Professional quality here means fast product finding, confident selection, accurate availability/pricing, legible photography, consistent layouts and reliable checkout. Brand art should amplify those tasks. The recommendations in STOREFRONT-PLAN.md are our proposed application of this research, not measured promises about conversion or rankings. The local references provide market vocabulary and useful entry points; none should become the complete design specification.
